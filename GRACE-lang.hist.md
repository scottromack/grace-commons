# GRACE lang — history

The dated record of how [GRACE lang](./GRACE-lang.md) got here, moved out of the grammar on 2026-10-22 because a reader does not need it to use the grammar today. The grammar keeps its rules, its live Watch list and its Changes section, Section 23; everything smaller is in `git log -- GRACE-lang.md`.

## Left the Watch list at the repeats sweep, 2026-10-22

The maintainer ruled on 2026-10-22 that the five names the Repeats rule had escalated are each stated once, four in the Execution Contract and one here, and the sweep that landed them removed every flag the five carried. Each had been cured one specification at a time by the Final Critiques before it was stated once.

- **The wiring declaration**, counted at twelve: the check that has an auditor identify which composing patterns a deployment wired in, which no record carries, moved from the conformance checks to the external checks — Actor Identity, Assignment, Capacity Constraint Enforcement, Legal Hold, Message Preference, Notification, Permissions, Retention Window, Subscription and Tamper Evidence, with Provenance and State Machine beside their external checks. Now Execution Contract Conformance 9.
- **The composed erasure**, counted at nine: a rule that no record leaves the database, or that no field of one changes, written with no actor beside a non-goal sending a purge or a scrub to a composing pattern — Selective Disclosure, Party Identity, Provisional Commitment, Observation, Capacity Constraint Enforcement, Message Preference, State Machine, Medication Order and Actor Identity, with Consent's Invariant 8.2 uncured. Now Execution Contract Database 6.
- **The admitted effect**, counted at five: a rule obliging what an action does, written with a subject that takes in a refused call — Event Log, Duplicate Prevention, Provisional Commitment, Provenance and State Machine, seven atoms writing their admitted calls in one form, and 172 sites behind `check.py`'s `W-unconditional-effect`, which the sweep retired. Now Execution Contract Read 4.
- **The refusal ban**, counted at five: a rule that an action must not refuse, written with no ground — Retention Window, Permissions, Duplicate Prevention and Observation narrowed to their grounds, Medication Order restated as what an action may change, and Legal Hold's Non-goal 4, Credential's Non-goal 37 and Approval Step's Non-goal 13 uncured. Now Execution Contract Refusal 1's second sentence.
- **The category reach**, counted at five: a rule whose subject takes in several actions, obliging an outcome one action's signature does not carry — Session, Capability, Party Identity, Provenance and Medication Order. Now Closed vocabulary 29 (v0.65).
