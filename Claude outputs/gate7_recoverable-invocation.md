FOUNDATIONAL FINDINGS (most severe first):

F1 — The `journal_fence`'s clock skew is unbudgeted, so Invariant 2 fails in the fully-conforming configuration [timing]
  WHERE: *Composes*, Instance capability requirements (`journal_fence`); Configuration `act_section` and `clock_skew_allowance`; [Reconcile] step 1; Invariant 2.
  DEFECT: The section handoff is decided on the **host's** clock (`expires_at`, and `take` succeeding the instant the lease runs out), while the `journal_fence` is judged "on the substrate's own clock" against that host-minted instant. The page budgets exactly this two-clock gap for `commit_fence` — "the sweep's abandon edge is the examine edge **plus the allowance once more**" — and budgets nothing for the journal fence: no edge is widened for it, and the direction of the "read under `clock_skew_allowance`" adjustment is never stated. A substrate clock lagging the host by up to `clock_skew_allowance` therefore admits a dead holder's append for a full allowance *after* the section has passed to the sweep, which is precisely the residue the fence was introduced to remove. Configuration's `clock_skew_allowance` entry also asserts flatly that a cross-seam comparison "never by itself decides a write", which is false of both fences — each admits or refuses a write on a two-seam comparison alone.
  FAILURE: Walkthrough numbers (`completion_bound` 30 s, `clock_skew_allowance` 2 s, `closure_latency` 5 s), substrate clock 2 s behind the section host. Invocation takes the section at t and its intent is stamped at ≈t. [Close] step 1 passes `remaining ≥ journal_write_bound`; the process is paused between that check and the issue — the check-then-act window the page itself names. The host lease expires at t+30; the sweep's `take` succeeds at t+30, re-reads (no closing), probes `committed`, writes `ledger.recovery_intended` and `ledger.disclosed` by ≈t+35. The paused invocation wakes at t+31.5 and issues its append; the substrate reads t+29.5, before the fence instant, so the write is admitted and lands. Two `ledger.disclosed` records name `inv-7f2` on a deployment that supplied every declared capability.
  FIX: Mint the fence instant as `expires_at − clock_skew_allowance` (refuse earlier, never later), or widen every sweep and [Resolve] *write* edge — not only the fenced abandon edge — to `completion_bound + 2 × clock_skew_allowance`; and correct the `clock_skew_allowance` entry's claim that no cross-seam comparison decides a write.

F2 — The lost-reply read-back concludes absence from a disclosed bound, not a fence [timing]
  WHERE: Primitive policies, "A `record_action` whose reply is lost … is never retried blind"; *Composes*, the read-your-writes clause; Configuration `journal_write_bound`.
  DEFECT: The no-duplicate conclusion after a lost reply rests entirely on `journal_write_bound` having elapsed since the issue. But `journal_write_bound` is defined as "the deployment's disclosed bound … with headroom" — an SLO — and this page's own doctrine, stated for `commit_fence`, is that such a bound decides nothing: "A relative request timeout is not a fence: it bounds the landing only of a write issued inside the lease, and nothing bounds when a paused process issues one." The only fence declared is at the **lease expiry**, which is strictly later than `issue + journal_write_bound` because every write is gated on `remaining ≥ journal_write_bound`. A write that lands late but still inside the lease is therefore admitted after the composition has already read absence and retried.
  FAILURE: [Close] step 3 issues the outcome append at T with 20 s of lease left and `journal_write_bound = 5 s`; the reply is lost. At T+5 the composition re-reads by `invocation_id`, sees nothing, and retries; the retry lands at T+6; the original append lands at T+7, 13 s inside the lease and so inside the fence. Two outcome records name one `invocation_id`; Generation acceptance check 2 reports `binding-duplicate` against a deployment that supplied every declared capability. The same sequence at [Open] step 4 yields two `<kind>.intended` records for one `invocation_id`, which no acceptance check detects at all.
  FIX: Make the fence per-write rather than per-lease — the `journal_fence` instant is `min(lease expiry, issue + journal_write_bound)` — and say so at the read-back, so that absence after the bound is final rather than merely likely.

F3 — [Close] and [Refuse] pre-check for outcomes only, so a sweep-written closing is not seen [timing]
  WHERE: [Close] step 2; [Refuse]; Invariant 2; Examples, *Regulator audit*.
  DEFECT: [Close]'s "proceed as landed" adopts only "an outcome record already naming this `invocation_id`". The sweep, however, closes an act with three record kinds: an outcome, `<kind>.abandoned`, or `<kind>.escalated` — and it escalates a *committed* act whenever `probe` answers `undecidable` (two store records match, or `pairing_datum = none`), and escalates every uncertain act on a fenceless kind, which the page says is the common case. Neither closing is an outcome record, so the waking invocation appends beside it. [Refuse] is worse: it has no re-read at all, only the `remaining` gate, and its collision case is the ordinary one, since a refusal that failed to land is exactly what makes the sweep write `abandoned`/`escalated`. Invariant 2 is stated only over *outcome* records and so does not forbid the pair, while the regulator scenario promises "exactly one **closing** record naming its `invocation_id`".
  FAILURE: Fenceless kind, `pairing_datum = disclosed_at`, two store records carrying that stamp. The invocation commits, passes [Close] step 1, and is paused. At `completion_bound + clock_skew_allowance` the sweep takes the section, probes `undecidable`, writes `ledger.escalated` naming `inv-7f2`, releases. The invocation wakes, step 2 finds no *outcome*, and appends `ledger.disclosed`. The act now carries an escalation and an outcome in no supersession shape the page defines ([Resolve]'s `supersedes` covers only an operator's outcome over an `abandoned`); [Read Invocation]'s `state` enum has no value for it; check 2 passes; the regulator's one-closing query fails.
  FIX: Have [Close] step 2 — and a new [Refuse] pre-check — re-read for *any* closing record naming the `invocation_id` (`<kind>.<outcome>`, `<kind>.refused`, `<kind>.abandoned`, `<kind>.escalated`), adopt or return the positioned failure accordingly, and restate Invariant 2 over closings rather than over outcomes.

F4 — Fenceless operation is simultaneously forbidden and specified, and the degradation it specifies is neither implemented nor observable [structural]
  WHERE: *Composes*, Instance capability requirements — the `journal_fence` degradation clause against the paragraph's closing sentence; [Close] step 2; Invariant 2 *Rests on:*; Terms, [Recording Failure].
  DEFECT: One paragraph says both that a substrate unable to offer `journal_fence` runs under a declared degradation ("Where the substrate cannot offer it, Invariant 2 degrades and the degradation must be declared … A deployment may not simply omit the fence and keep the invariant") and that "A deployment that supplies the first three has a conforming reconciliation; one that does not has none and this composition does not start" — the fence being the third. Three further sections then specify fenceless behaviour in detail. Independently, the degradation cannot be used: it quantifies Invariant 2 over "writes whose issuers were not paused past their lease", a set no auditor can enumerate from the records, and it assigns resolution to [Read Invocation] ("resolves by lowest `sequence_number`") and reporting to `compliance_surface` — neither of which appears in [Read Invocation]'s own section or in any step of any action.
  FAILURE: A deployment on today's library — the page states no atom declares a store fence, and Audit Trail's `record_action(action_ref, actor_ref, credential, data)` carries no deadline — reads this paragraph to decide whether it may start. Half forbids it; half specifies its behaviour; the specified behaviour names a duplicate-resolution rule the named action does not implement and an invariant an auditor cannot evaluate. A generator produces either a composition that refuses to start on every real substrate, or one whose `binding-duplicate` handling exists nowhere.
  FIX: Decide one. Either fenceless is non-conforming — delete the degradation and the fenceless arms of [Close] step 2 and Invariant 2 — or it is a declared degraded mode, in which case restate degraded Invariant 2 over an observable set (at most one outcome, or a `binding-duplicate` surfaced), and put the lowest-`sequence_number` resolution into [Read Invocation]'s steps and the surfacing into [Reconcile]'s.

F5 — Nothing relates `compensation_window` to `retention_period`, so Invariant 4's liveness is unmeetable and undetectable [timing]
  WHERE: Configuration `compensation_window` and `retention_period`; Invariant 4 liveness; Invariant 7; Generation acceptance checks 3, 4, and 7.
  DEFECT: The instance-start check is the five-term inequality alone. The sweep's *upper* edge is `recorded_at + retention_period` and Invariant 7 forbids examining past it, but no entry, invariant, or check constrains the window against that horizon. Where the horizon is shorter than the window plus the examine edge, an orphan leaves the sweep's range before the window elapses and is closed by nobody — and check 7 removes it from checks 1–3, so no auditor ever sees the breach. Invariant 4 then promises closure "within `compensation_window` of its `recorded_at`" over a set the sweep provably cannot reach, and Invariant 4's safety arm ("never a quiet inconsistency") fails with it once the payload is destroyed.
  FAILURE: `completion_bound` 30 s, `closure_latency` 5 s, `clock_skew_allowance` 2 s, `reconciliation_cadence` 60 s, `run_bound` 120 s, `compensation_window` 600 s, `retention_period` 60 s. The inequality gives 244 s < 600 s, so the instance starts. An orphan created at t becomes examinable at t+32 s; the next cadence run may not reach it until t+92 s; by then `recorded_at + retention_period` has passed and [Reconcile] step 1 drops it permanently. No closing record ever lands, and check 3 never counts it because check 7 exempts it.
  FIX: Add a per-act-kind instance-start check — `completion_bound + clock_skew_allowance + compensation_window < retention_period`, strictly — refusing to start otherwise, and state it beside the liveness inequality in Configuration, Invariant 4, and Generation acceptance check 4.

F6 — An intent of an unbound act kind is promised a surfacing no step produces [structural]
  WHERE: *Bindings*, opening paragraph; Configuration `bindings`; [Reconcile] steps 1 and 5; Generation acceptance check 3.
  DEFECT: The page promises that "an intent of a kind the table no longer binds is surfaced on `compliance_surface` and never closed, since nothing can probe it, and Generation acceptance check 3 exempts it". No step produces that surfacing. [Reconcile] step 1 rebuilds `open_invocations` "filtered to this act kind's `<kind>.intended` records" and bounds the pass by that kind's `completion_bound` and `retention_period` — none of which exist for an unbound kind — and step 5 surfaces only intents "this run examined or skipped". The exemption is therefore real and the report is not: the intents are invisible to the sweep, invisible to the checks, and the only remaining guard is Configuration's advisory "a deployment unbinds a kind only after resolving every open intent of it".
  FAILURE: An operator unbinds *transfer custody* while three invocations of it are open. The sweep has no filter for the kind and no edges to compute; step 5 surfaces nothing; check 3 exempts all three; check 7 does not reach them. Three irreversible acts go permanently unaccounted for, with no record and no alert — the state Invariant 4's safety arm claims is "never a quiet inconsistency".
  FIX: Give [Reconcile] a step that enumerates records whose `kind` field (which the composition writes into every record it appends) the `bindings` table does not serve, and surfaces them on `compliance_surface` under the instance's own retention edge; or make unbinding a kind that still has open intents a refused configuration change.

F7 — The walkthrough's inequality arithmetic is wrong, and wrong in the direction of the formula the page repudiates [structural]
  WHERE: Examples, *Walkthrough — a disclosure, three ways*, the parenthesised check of Invariant 4's inequality.
  DEFECT: `30 + max(30, 5) + 4 + 60 + 120` is **244 s**, not the 274 s the page states. 274 is what the superseded six-term form gives — with a second `completion_bound` added — which is exactly the form Configuration's `compensation_window` entry says "this page said in an earlier round and is wrong wherever `closure_latency` exceeds the act's bound".   FAILURE: An auditor clearing check 4 against the walkthrough computes 244 and cannot reconcile it with the stated 274. The only reconciliation available from the page's own history is to re-add the second `completion_bound` — restoring the understatement the corrected term exists to remove, and, on any kind where `closure_latency > completion_bound`, producing a different wrong answer in the other direction.
  FIX: Replace 274 s with 244 s.
