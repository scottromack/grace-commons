# Community

The participation contract, in three sentences. **Contributing starts smaller than you think:** the cheapest real contribution is reporting an ambiguity you found while reading — a *finding* — and the library's first external issue produced a canonicalized methodology section, so the loop demonstrably works. **Decisions bind through review, not conversation:** findings route through the pressure-testing channel, fixes land in the pattern's Lineage notes, and nothing changes canon mid-build — the spec changes only through a review pass. **The license is Apache 2.0** — the patterns belong to everyone, which is the point of a commons.

How to contribute is the first page below. How admission and sealing will be governed as the library matures, and the architectural decisions deliberately still open, are in [The Library](./corpus.md): [Governance](./governance.md) and [Open Questions](./open-questions.md).

Three pages say how we treat each other and what to do when something goes wrong: the [code of conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), the [security policy](./SECURITY.md) and the [accessibility statement](./ACCESSIBILITY.md). How we talk about the work has rules of its own: [Marketing, the Grace way](./marketing.md).

Questions go to [Discussions](https://github.com/scottromack/grace-commons/discussions/categories/q-a), in the Q&A category; findings go in an [issue](https://github.com/scottromack/grace-commons/issues/new/choose). To talk with the people building Grace, join us on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/U2qdd7tR2): questions and findings keep their homes above, so the record stays on GitHub.
