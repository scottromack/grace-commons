# Marketing, the Grace way

The rules for anything said in Grace's name outside the library: a front-door page, a post, a deck, a demo. There is tension among them, not contradiction.

## Core principles (apply everywhere)

- **Claim and proof never drift apart**

  Example: Every assertion in a post, deck, or demo is paired with the exact evidence that backs it – no lag, no hand-waving.

- **Plain words for business readers**

  Example: No jargon: “one piece at a time, while everything keeps running”, not “strangler pattern”.

- **Outcome, not internals**

  Example: No Mayor, Readers, Judge or council in the posts; those live on the site for the few who click “how does it work?”

- **No theater: every demo is a real run**

  Example: Live source, live data, live result. No staged screens or pre-recorded “success.”

- **Uncomfortable truth, then the fix**

  Example: Name the real problem first – even if it stings – then show the concrete remedy.

- **What would Jobs do**

  Example: Before shipping the message, ask whether the simplest, most elegant version has already been found.

## Execution rules (how we ship the work)

- **Pitch fits in a few minutes, or it's dead**

  Example: If the core claim cannot be stated and proven in under five minutes, cut until it can.

- **One idea per post**

  Example: A single LinkedIn or X post carries exactly one claim, one proof, one ask.

- **Wide net, still targeted**

  Example: Cast broadly enough to find the right people, then speak only to the ones who match the actual buyer.

- **Never repeat**

  Example: Once a point has been made cleanly, move on. Repetition is treated as noise.
