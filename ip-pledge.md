# Our IP pledge

Grace wants the commons: the patterns every business shares, such as audit, retention, sign-in and recoverable acts, which no company should have to write again. The part of your business that is yours, the pricing rule, the fraud score, the eligibility logic no one else has, is your gold, and it stays yours. It never enters the corpus unless you choose to contribute it. Grace helps you find it, write it down clearly and claim it in your name. We hold no part of it, and we are not here to take it. We are here to help you protect it, and to build the commons around it.

## What claiming means

Claiming is a clear, dated record in your name of what is yours. Copyright covers the written words, not the idea behind them, and publishing is your choice: some will want their edge public and some will keep it private. This page promises no patent and no legal protection.

## What the hosted service keeps

Before this page goes live with a hosted service, the service must keep one promise: a client's private specification is read only by models that do not keep or train on prompts. No free or stealth model reads it, because those providers may log what they are sent; only zero-retention routes do. If the council cannot route that way today, the hosted service is for public patterns until it can.
