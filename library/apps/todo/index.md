## What it is

Shared Todo is an app: an author sends a set of to-dos to a team, in the author's order of priority, and the team sees them, claims them and checks them off; the first check-off wins and who did it is saved. It is built from ten atoms wired by their own need and offer lines, one bond instance and one counted line of glue. It is the first app in the bond shape; the composition [Shared Todo](../../compositions/shared-todo.md) stays as it stands, the frozen reference, until the review of this page.

<details markdown="block">
<summary>WHY</summary>

The app is the 1.0 target (Scott, 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-25): one whole app built from the tree, end to end. Every need of its atoms but one meets exactly one offer of its atoms, so the bonds form and the app writes nothing for them ([Bonds](../../bonds.md), Bond 8); the one need that meets several offers is the one bond the app writes (Bond 7). ## Jobs

Each job has one acceptance check, the test that says the app does it.

1. **Hand out work in priority order.** Check: the team reads the list in the author's order.
2. **Know who is on what.** Check: a claimed task shows its assignee; a reassign keeps the history; a reassign to someone the list is not shared with is refused.
3. **Know what is done and by whom.** Check: a completed task shows its completer and stays crossed out a week; a second check-off is refused: Task refuses it for complete, and the app answers `not-known` for claim, recall, reassign and move (App check 5).
4. **Keep it the author's list.** Check: a non-author's rename, delete list, add, edit, reorder, delete, recall, reassign or invite is refused.

Two answers settle what the jobs leave open (Scott, 2026-11-25 7:15 PM Central):

- **No reopen.** A done task is a recorded fact with a week's display life (the window of one week that read list passes to [Task](../../atoms/task.idd)'s Read): a second check-off is refused, claim, recall, reassign and move task refuse a done task with `not-known` (App check 5), and the journal keeps who completed it. The app offers no reopen.
- **The author alone recalls or reassigns a claim.** The author may take a claim back ([Assignment](../../atoms/assignment.idd)'s Recall For Task) or give it to someone the list is shared with (its Reassign, through its Active For; App check 2), and the history is kept; a non-author may do neither, and a reassign to someone the list is not shared with is refused. Reassign is an author action, not a glue line.

## Atoms

Ten, each used as it stands:

- **[Task](../../atoms/task.idd)** — the tasks of a list: add, edit, complete, delete; mints the task id; saves the completer.
- **[List](../../atoms/list.idd)** — makes a list, mints its list id, holds its name and its author.
- **[Ordering](../../atoms/ordering.idd)** — one list's sequence: the author's order of priority, a new task at the bottom.
- **[Assignment](../../atoms/assignment.idd)** — who is on a task: assign, recall, reassign, with its history.
- **[Permissions](../../atoms/permissions.idd)** — who may do what on a list: grant, revoke, permitted.
- **[Invitation](../../atoms/invitation.idd)** — bringing a person into a list: initiate, accept, decline.
- **[Actor Identity](../../atoms/actor-identity.idd)** — who someone is; offers the actor id the app's actions carry.
- **[Session](../../atoms/session.idd)** — who is calling; mints the session id.
- **[Credential](../../atoms/credential.idd)** — the proof checked at sign-in.
- **[Event Log](../../atoms/event-log.idd)** — the history: the journal of task, assignment and invitation events.

Not in the app (the design of 2026-11-24): Capability (Permissions covers sharing), Party Identity (a team is a Permissions group in the first version), Restorable Delete (Task's plain delete in the first version). Every call of the atoms that no row, check or answer of this page names is left to no action of this version, and four on purpose: Actor Identity's Mint Actor Id and Credential's Register (the actor id and the credential of a person who signs in are provisioned outside the app, and the app takes an actor id as given; each such actor id is minted outside the app's actions (App permission 6), before its credential is registered (App permission 8)), Permissions' Revoke (a grant is never taken back: a member stays a member, and a deleted list's grants reach nothing) and Invitation's Revoke (a pending invitation ends by accept, decline, its expiry or the deletion of its list).

### The app's permission need

The app's gate is one rule set, stated as the app's own need line (Bonds Bond 13, Term app page need): the author may do everything on their list; anyone the list is shared with may read it, claim and complete, nothing else. The scope a check asks for is a list id paired with one of two standings, `author` or `member`, written as the list id, a colon and the standing (`<list id>:author`), the same bytes in every grant and every check; making a list gives its maker both, and accepting an invitation to it gives `member`: invite offers the scope `member` of the list, and the follow-up on accept grants it (Bonds Follow-up 1).

Term need permission: subject actor id; scope opaque; actions rename list, delete list, add task, edit task, move task, delete task, recall, reassign, invite, read list, claim, complete; calls permitted; arms permitted, denied.

Term scope length: the length of the scope `member` of a list (`<list id>:member`) in Unicode code points, counted PER Execution Contract Input 2; the scope `author` of the list has the same length.

```
App permission 1: rename list, delete list, add task, edit task, move task, delete task, recall, reassign and invite MUST be gated on the scope `author` of the list the action is on.
App permission 2: read list, claim and complete MUST be gated on the scope `member` of the list the action is on.
App permission 3: make list, accept, decline, sign in and sign out MUST NOT be gated.
App permission 4: The host MUST supply EVERY list id at a scope length that DOES NOT EXCEED Permissions' string cap AND DOES NOT EXCEED Invitation's string cap.
App permission 5: The deployment MUST NOT change Invitation's string cap for the life of Invitation's instance.
App permission 6: The deployment MUST mint EVERY actor id a credential or a session names as its principal by Actor Identity's Mint Actor Id, on the one Actor Identity instance the app's actor id needs meet (Bonds Bond 9).
App permission 7: The host MUST supply EVERY list id, EVERY task id, EVERY actor id AND EVERY credential id at a length that DOES NOT EXCEED EVERY string cap of an atom of the app that applies to an input carrying the id (Execution Contract Input 2); for a list id App permission 4 governs, the tighter bound, since it leaves room for the `:member` suffix, and this rule governs every other id.
App permission 8: The deployment MUST NOT call Credential's Register with a principal reference BEFORE Actor Identity's Mint Actor Id answers the principal reference as an actor id, on the one Actor Identity instance App permission 6 names.
```

Make list's grant is an effect of the generated action, not a gate: once List's Make List answers, make list calls Permissions' Grant of `author` and `member` on the new list to the caller, and no check is asked before it (App permission 3).

The subject of every check is the caller (Bonds permission 3), the actor id that [Session](../../atoms/session.idd)'s Validate answers for the call's session. Every input that names the acting party (the author, the inviter, the completer, the accepting identity, the revoker, and the assignee a claim binds) is that same actor id, never a value the call carries (Bonds Key 19); the subject of the grant that follows an accept is the accepting identity Invitation's Accept answers, so it is that actor id too (Bonds Follow-up 2). Two values the call carries are no acting party: a reassign's new assignee, which App check 2 admits only where it answers permitted, and a sign in's principal, which Credential's Verify checks against the material the call carries and which grants no authority to act (Bonds Key 19). App check 2 is a call of Permissions' Check in reassign's flow after the gate, asked of the new assignee for no gated action, as make list's Grant is a call, and no permission check of the app's need (Bonds Term permission check): the gate of reassign is App permission 1's, asked of the caller (Bonds permission 2, Bonds permission 3).

### The app's checks and answers

An action that acts on a task names its task id and the list id of its gate, and the gate is asked of that list. Edit task, delete task, move task, claim, complete, recall and reassign each run App check 1 after the gate; Task's Read of the list, with the window of one week, is the read App check 1 makes of the task id; Task's offer names Read Task as its existence read (Bonds Key 26), and App check 1 runs Task's Read of the list in its place, which answers a task only while the list shows it, so a done task past its week answers `not-known` as a task id no task carries does (Bonds Key 21 to Key 25); Actor Identity's offer names Read Actor, and no action runs a key check on an actor id, since App permission 6 and App permission 8 have the deployment mint every actor id a credential names before the credential is recorded, and the principal of every session is the principal of a credential Verify answers (Bonds Follow-up 2). Claim, recall, reassign and move task run App check 5 right after App check 1, on the task App check 1's read answers: a done task inside its week is refused there, as one past its week is refused by App check 1, and Task's own Edit refuses a done task as not-editable. Reassign runs App check 2 right after App check 5: the new assignee must be someone the list is shared with. Add task, invite and read list run App check 3 after the gate, since the list id they carry is a key the app's write or read meets (Bonds Key 21). Sign in runs App check 4 after Credential's Verify answers verified and before Session's Issue, since the credential id Issue writes is a key the app's write meets (Bonds Key 21): that read is the key check of the id, and no input of Issue comes from it, Issue's inputs being the values of Verify's answer and the app's `shared todo` (Bonds Follow-up 2, Follow-up 4, Follow-up 8). The `not-known` of App check 1, App check 3 and App check 5 stands where Execution Contract Refusal 3 puts it, and each check's read comes before the atom's call it guards, in the action's one transaction (Bonds Key 22), since Task's Delete ends the task id that App check 1 asks Task's Read to answer, and a read after it would answer `not-known` on every delete task.

```
App check 1: edit task, delete task, move task, claim, complete, recall and reassign MUST confirm, in the action's one transaction, that Task's Read of the list id the call carries, with a window of one week (Term one week), answers the task id, and MUST answer `not-known` and commit nothing where it does not (Bonds Key 24; Execution Contract Refusal 3).
App check 2: reassign MUST confirm, in the action's one transaction, that Permissions' Check of the new assignee's actor id on the scope `member` of the list the call carries answers permitted, and MUST answer `denied` and commit nothing where it does not.
App check 3: add task, invite and read list MUST confirm, in the action's one transaction, that List's Read List of the list id the call carries answers the list, and MUST answer `not-known` and commit nothing where it answers none (Bonds Key 21, Bonds Key 24; Execution Contract Refusal 3).
App check 4: sign in MUST confirm, in the action's one transaction and before Session's Issue, that Credential's Read of the credential id Verify answers answers the credential, and MUST answer `not-known` and commit nothing where it answers none (Bonds Key 21, Bonds Key 22, Bonds Key 24; Execution Contract Refusal 3).
App check 5: claim, recall, reassign and move task MUST answer `not-known` and commit nothing where the task App check 1's read answers is a done task (a done task carries Task's Completion Instant, present only while the task is done; Execution Contract Refusal 3).
App answer 1: an action called with a session whose Validate answers invalid MUST answer invalid with Validate's failure as it stands, MUST call no atom's action beyond Session's Validate and MUST commit nothing.
App answer 2: sign in, where Credential's Verify answers failed-verification, MUST answer failed-verification with Verify's failure as it stands and MUST call no Session action (Bonds Follow-up 1).
```

Term one week: the window the app passes to Task's Read, 604800 seconds (seven days of 86400 seconds each, no calendar and no timezone); a done task lies inside it where its completion instant does not precede now by more than that, the instant exactly one week back included (Task's Read; Execution Contract Query 2).

A done task past its week stands on no list the app reads, so every action that runs App check 1 answers `not-known` on it, a second check-off included (refused either way). Task's Add still counts such a task in the active set of its list, so add task of its description answers `duplicate-active` while it stands, and only delete list ends it. Every other answer of an action is the answer of the atom's call that gave it, each rejection passed through unchanged (Execution Contract Constituent failure 1 to Constituent failure 3), with `denied` for a failed gate and for a reassign App check 2 refuses, Check's own answer.
