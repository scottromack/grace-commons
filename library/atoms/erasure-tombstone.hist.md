# Erasure Tombstone — history

The dated record of how [Erasure Tombstone](./erasure-tombstone.md) got here, begun on 2026-11-13 when the spec's tombstones moved out of it because a reader does not need them to use the spec today; everything smaller is in `git log -- atoms/erasure-tombstone.md`.

## Decisions


- **2026-11-01 — Drafted as the extraction nine compositions name.** *Chose:* one record per destruction — destroyed pattern, destroyed id, destruction instant, authority kind and reference, actor — with no content of the destroyed record and no state, three actions (record, read one, list a window), the destroying pattern calling [Record Destruction] in the transaction that deletes, two authority kinds every deployment keeps and others it may add, and stated default caps a deployment may override. The record is named *erasure tombstone* in full, since GRACE-lang already calls a deleted rule's label a tombstone. *Over:* a tombstone that also deletes, judges lawfulness, or keeps any field of the destroyed record. *Because:* Scott's interview of 2026-11-01 and his ruling the same day that a host's bounds take a stated default a deployment may override; the atom carries exactly what the nine compositions' extraction-pending facts need, and nothing a deletion would have to forget.

- **2026-11-26 — The atom converted to the bond shape, batch 6 of the converter: it mints no id, offers nothing, needs nothing, and names no other pattern.** *Chose:* the tree order, every label kept and none renumbered. An erasure tombstone is identified by the destroyed pattern and the destroyed id (Identity 1): it extends the destroyed record, so Bonds Key 11 asks no minted id of it and Key 14 forbids offering an id it does not mint, as on Owed Record. No need: the actor reference is never interpreted (Identity 4). The five `MUST compose <pattern>` Non-goals (2, 4, 10, 12, 15) tombstoned to the Non-goal that states what the atom does not do; Non-goal 6 and 8 compose an erasure coordination and a shred pattern by job; the WHY, the scenarios, the owners NOTE and the composing NOTE say each job, not a pattern. Labelled rules 97 before, 92 after; 577 lines before, 600 after. *Over:* the drafted erasure tombstone id offer and actor id need. *Because:* unit `atom: Erasure Tombstone, converted` (batch 6, 2 of 5).
