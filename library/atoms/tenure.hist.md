# Tenure — history

The dated record of how [Tenure](./tenure.md) got here, moved out of the spec because a reader does not need it to use the spec today. Everything smaller is in `git log -- atoms/tenure.md`.

## Decisions

- **2026-11-16 — Drafted through the Grace connector mirroring Reaction.** *Chose:* one tenure for one person in one role in one thing, dated, with upcoming, held and ended worked out from the dates and withdrawn set by hand *Over:* a role kept on the person, or Assignment's one holder with no dates of the business's own *Because:* several people share a role, one person holds several, and a departure learned late must land on the day it happened.
- **2026-11-16 — Tenure, not Role.** *Chose:* Tenure for the record, and role for the part the record holds *Over:* Role as the pattern's name *Because:* the record is one person's time in a role, not the role, and the forum plan keeps Role for what a role permits; tenure appears nowhere in the library.
- **2026-11-16 — No Active.** *Chose:* upcoming, held and ended from the dates, and withdrawn by hand *Over:* Active and Withdrawn, the pair Communication uses *Because:* Active means has not ended in the library, as in Assignment, and an ended tenure still counts in every history.
- **2026-11-16 — The end date is the first day not held.** *Chose:* an end date that is the first day the role is not held *Over:* a last day held *Because:* one date ends one tenure and begins the next, so a handover leaves no gap and no overlap.
