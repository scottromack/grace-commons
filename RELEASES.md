# Releases

Grace Commons is versioned by minor version on the way to 1.0.0: 0.1 the shake-out (every composition into atoms, bonds and glue), 0.2 prose strictified and the bonds rename, 0.3 the grammar and the round trip, 0.4 Lean statements reviewed and proofs cached, 0.5 codegen and tests from the tree, 1.0.0 the shared to-do app built end to end from the tree. Atoms keep their own semantic versions. Each entry is generated from the commit subjects between two tags (`assets/release-notes.py` in grace-council), never written by hand.

## v0.0.0 — 2026-11-23

The baseline before the full stack: 74 patterns (48 atoms, 26 compositions), 52 of them done (43 atoms, 9 compositions), as the Status lines read at this tag. Versions before it are the whole-number history (V1, v2, v3), kept in the roadmap and the history files as they were written. Each later minor version is tagged by the unit that closes its milestone.
