## Outcome

- GAP rows: **none**. Owed: Identity 5; Operation 47 through 65 ([Read]'s query); Operation 66 through 77 (malformed and over-cap inputs).
- by-construction flags on load-bearing invariants: none.
- Under-specified, not cured (no rule changes, no round owed): (1) Invariant 3 holds at now only on a clock that never goes back: set back, a revoked or expired record reads granted again. The page hands the clock's monotonicity to Execution Contract Logic confinement 7 only through its deleted Clock semantics rows, not beside Invariant 3, and Invariant 3's words do not tie "revoked" and "expired" to now (against a future at time, an expired answer does turn revoked when a withdrawal lands before the expiry instant, as the Summary intends). (2) No labelled rule keeps a recorded withdrawal: Invariant 1 freezes the grant fields only, and the revoking actor, revocation reason and revocation instant are "immutable thereafter" only in the Term entry for [Revoke]; Operation 18 keeps them by refusal only while now does not precede the recorded revocation instant, so a [Revoke] whose clock reading falls before it finds the record not withdrawn and writes over the first withdrawal. (3) "A revoked consent record" (Invariant 4, State 7) reads two ways, the derived state (Term state) or the stored state [Revoke] wrote; on the derived reading a record stands in revoked only by carrying a revocation instant, so Invariant 4.3 holds by definition, and the model reads both (the page's own NOTE on the state's term kind).
- Result: **all Invariants covered** — 2026-11-11. *Lineage entry: "Coverage cross-check 2026-11-11 — every Invariant covered by a named check, each broken by its twin; Identity 5, [Read]'s query and the malformed and over-cap inputs owed."*
