## Step 3 — bound saturation

Per Lineage: `MaxA=3` assignment slots for a single task → 47 reachable states. Three slots is the minimum to exercise the full assign → reassign → recall chain without slot exhaustion. Appears adequate.

Alloy (2026-11-11): `assignment.als` checks at `for 4 but 5 Assignment, 3 Call` (three calls build a version three deep, assign → reassign → recall or reassign the successor; four references, ids and instants give distinct actors and an instant before, at and after another). Raised once to `for 5 but 6 Assignment, 4 Call` on a scratch copy: the same verdicts, 11 checks UNSAT and 10 runs SAT. A copy without the fact `ClockNeverRunsBackward` breaks `A_Invariant_8_1` and `A_Invariant_8_2` alone (no guard of the page compares instants; the deployment's clock holds them); a copy without `HostSuppliesAnIdToOneCall` breaks `A_Invariant_6_1` alone (no rule of the page makes the host's id fresh; Execution Contract Logic confinement 16 does, and the page does not cite it).
