## Step 2 — coverage matrix

The Alloy model takes one call of any action, any inputs, and the answer its action gives (a result, an order, parts or a refusal), and checks what that answer must carry as a value; the rounded product and each part's amount are any whole number there, computed by the TLA+ model. The TLA+ model writes each action as a function from the call's inputs to its answer, makes every call its bounds admit, and then the calls around it: the composing pattern writing its own records and the same call made again, the same [Multiply] or [Split] with the money negated, and after an [Add] that answered, the [Subtract] of the same other money from its result. ## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At `for 4 but 5 Int, 3 seq` (the shipped bound): all 5 checks UNSAT, all 14 runs SAT.
- At `for 5 but 6 Int, 4 seq` on a scratch copy: the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑ (about 16 s). One call already shows every answer shape: a result, an order, parts, every refusal, and a pair of currencies a normalizer would merge; a fifth atom and a sixth bit add larger amounts and longer share lists, no new shape.

TLA+: a number cap of 4 and a part cap of 3, every call they admit (amounts and numerators -5 to 5, denominators -1 to 5, up to four shares of 0 to 5): 529,157 states, every check holding. On scratch copies outside the repository: a number cap of 5 (part cap 3), 1,035,161 states, and a part cap of 4 (number cap 4), 2,488,709 states, the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑ (58 s and 137 s on two workers). A larger cap adds larger amounts and shares, a fourth part adds longer lists; neither adds a refusal, a rounding case or a sign the shipped bound lacks. Past the bounds the five laws are proved, not sampled: TLAPS discharges Invariant 2.1, 3.1, 4.1, 6.1 and 6.2 for every amount, rate, rounding and number of shares.

Three notes for the next reader. (1) An answer's currency is stated only by Invariant 1.1 and 1.2 (and Term negation for [Negate]): Operation 5, 7, 16, 21 and 22 name the amount alone, so those two Invariants act as the rule for the currency, not as a consequence of other rules; a reader who changes them changes the actions. (3) Invariant 6.1 and 6.2 speak only where the first call answers; where it is refused the negated call meets the same refusal (each refusal reads a size, never a sign), which the rules give and the models do not check separately. The open item 2026-11-13-a (a currency's length) is untouched: neither model bounds a currency.
