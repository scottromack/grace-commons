## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At `for 4` (the shipped bound): all 19 checks UNSAT, all 7 runs SAT.
- At `for 5` on a scratch copy: the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑ (about 5 minutes). Four calls already build a line of three, a called spot with the same person waiting behind, a left spot, a closed waitlist with an unserved spot, an offering started again, and case variants side by side; a fifth call adds a longer history and no new shape of a record.

TLA+: two offering references, two person references, a blank and an over-the-cap reference, two waitlist ids, two spot ids, two clock readings: 12,983 states, every check holding, about 7 s.
