The model has two parts. Part A: every state of the workflows and start events, the open intents, steps and assignments, the opening, decision and firing events and moot records, and the opening leg's openings and out-of-band findings that admitted writes leave (a start, an opening, a decision, a firing with its moot cascade, the opening and firing legs). Part B: the four indexes as stored (each entry written after its event lands, Action wiring 13, any write allowed to fail, Action wiring 14), as their named rebuilds reproduce them (Composition state 11 through 14), and after a rebuild (Composition state 15). Five facts, each from outside the page: a gate key is a key, Event Log's sequence numbers, Retention Window purging the oldest first, the clock's edge (an examined step's open intent is Retained), and Approval Step refusing only a cascade's withdraw.
