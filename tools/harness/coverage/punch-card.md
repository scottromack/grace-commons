## Outcome

- GAP rows: **none**. Owed: none.
- by-construction flags on load-bearing invariants: none.
- Under-specified, not cured (no rule changes, no round owed): no counterexample in either correct model, and no rule the models needed that the page does not give. Two notes for the next reader. Operation 9 and 12 test a refusal by equality (stamps EQUALS / DOES NOT EQUAL the goal), while the case table says a stamp lands and a redeem is refused when the stamps are "below the goal"; the two readings agree only because Invariant 1.2 holds, and both models use both (equality for the refusals, below for a landing stamp), so a reader who changes one should change the other. The redemptions have no ceiling on the page (a whole count, raised by every redeem), so the TLA+ model bounds its search with `RedemptionBound`, not with a rule. The page states no eventually-property, so the TLA+ model checks safety only.
