## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At `for 4 but 5 Call, 4 Line, 4 Int` (the shipped bound): all 13 checks UNSAT, all 9 runs SAT.
- At `for 5 but 6 Call, 5 Line, 4 Int` on a scratch copy: the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑ (about 15 minutes of solver time). Five calls already build two lines on one cart, an item taken off and added again, a cleared cart and a cart at its cap; a sixth call adds a longer history and no new shape of a record.

TLA+: two cart ids and three line ids. With two item references and one line per item per cart, three line ids reach every shape the page names: two lines on one cart, a line on each cart, an item taken out and added again under a new line id beside another, and a cart cleared with lines on the other. The shop's prices take every value for both items at every point, and both accepted currencies are opened. A third cart id adds states and no shape no check reads.
