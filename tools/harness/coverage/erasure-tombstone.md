## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At `for 4 but 4 DB, 3 Call, 3 Tombstone, 5 Text` (the shipped bound): all 15 checks UNSAT, all 7 runs SAT.
- At `for 5 but 5 DB, 4 Call, 4 Tombstone, 6 Text` on a scratch copy: the same verdicts (about 6 s) → **saturated** ☑. Three calls build every shape the page admits (each authority kind, one id in two patterns, two ids a normalizer would merge, two destructions at one instant); a fourth adds length, not shape.

TLA+ (the shipped bound): two tombstone slots, the clock 0..1, a page size of 1 or 2: 37,202 states, about 29 s. With three slots: 310,610 states, about 409 s, the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑. A third slot adds a third tombstone beside the two a pattern can hold here, so a listing filters one pattern out while it pages through the other; no check reads a shape two slots lack.
