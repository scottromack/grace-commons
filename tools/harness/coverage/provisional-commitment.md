## Step 3 — bound saturation

`PlacedAt=1`, `ExpiresAt=2`, `MaxClock=3` → **12 states**, all invariants hold. `MaxClock > ExpiresAt > PlacedAt` ensures: the within-window guard can fire, expiry is reachable (Expire enabled at clock ∈ {2, 3}), and confirm/release at clock ∈ {1} are covered. The clock is modeled as an advancing input, so the raw state count grows with `MaxClock` while the behavior space saturates once the clock crosses `ExpiresAt`; all distinct behavioral interleavings (confirm/release within window, expire at or after window) are present at `MaxClock=3`. Semantically saturated at `MaxClock=3`.

Alloy (2026-11-11): `provisional-commitment.als` checks at `for 4 but 3 Commitment, 3 Call` (three versions and three calls, four strings, integers -8 through 7: enough for a place with a resolve and the second resolve a twin admits, or for a released commitment and a second one on its resource). The scope was shrunk from `for 4`: there the correct model took about 4 s, but `provisional-commitment-buggy-resolve-unguarded.als` (unbounded resolve chains) took about 5 minutes inside the page audit on a loaded machine, where at the smaller scope it takes about 8 s.
