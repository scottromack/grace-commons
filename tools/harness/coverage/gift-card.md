## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At `for 4 but 4 Int` (the shipped bound): all 12 checks UNSAT, all 7 runs SAT.
- At `for 5 but 5 Int` on a scratch copy: the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑ (about 108 s). Four calls already build a gift card spent twice beside a second card, a card at its cap, two cards in two currencies and two codes a normalizer would merge; a fifth call adds a longer history and no new shape of a record.

TLA+: two card ids, three card codes, values 2 and 3, amounts 0 to 3: 21,071 states, every check holding, about 3 s. At three card ids, four codes, values 1 to 3 and amounts 0 to 4 on a scratch copy: 3,933,986 states, the same verdicts, about 363 s on 4 workers → **saturated** ☑. Two cards already reach a spend on one beside the other, a code no card carries, an amount of zero, within, equal to and over the balance, and a card spent to zero.

- Audit of the page's models (`node audit.mjs --only atoms/gift-card`): 30 models, 2 HOLDS, 28 CAUGHT, 29 checks, 0 red; its TWIN PER CHECK names a twin for every check.

## Outcome

- GAP rows: **none**. Owed: Identity 11, Operation 14 and 15.
- by-construction flags on load-bearing invariants: none.
- Under-specified, not cured (no rule changes, no round owed): Operation 1 and 3 are judged at [Issue] only, and the accepted currencies and the card caps are deployment settings; the page says nothing of a gift card whose currency a deployment later stops accepting, or whose value a lowered cap no longer admits, which [Check Balance], [Spend] and [Read] then go on serving.
