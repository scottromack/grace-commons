## Step 2 — coverage matrix

The Alloy model builds any number of [Fanout] calls, each with its inputs, the seam's clock reading and drawn id, its subscribers_for call (the scope asked, how it came back, the references answered, the instant the database fixed them), its Notification creates (the arguments, how each came back, the record each committed), and its answer; records no fanout wrote may stand beside them. The TLA+ model runs two fanouts over two subscribers one after another, a step at a time — take the id, read, one create per subscriber (answered, refused, or unanswered with its record committed or not), answer — while subscribers subscribe and cancel, the delivery layer delivers or fails one record, a read fails, and a fanout crashes.

## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At `for 3 but 4 Int, 2 Fanout` (the shipped bound): all 20 checks UNSAT, all 9 runs SAT.
- At `for 4 but 4 Int, 3 Fanout` (on a scratch copy outside the repo): the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑. Two fanouts and three creates already build every shape a check reads: two results of one scope, a partial failure beside a created record, an unrecordable create whose record committed, an empty answer, a failed read, a refused call.

TLA+: at two references, three records and two fanouts (the shipped bound): 76,945 states. At three references and four records on a scratch copy: 11,560,519 states in about 6 minutes, every check holding. The count grows with the bound, so this is not saturation in the template's sense; it is the same verdict over every shape the larger bound adds (a third subscriber joining or cancelling mid fan-out, a fourth record). Two references and three records already reach every shape a check reads: a create failing beside a created record of the same fanout, one subscriber cancelling and another joining after the read, a second fanout's create beside a first fanout's pending record, a delivery of one record beside its sibling, a crash partway.

## Outcome

- GAP rows: **none**. Owed: Primitive policy 7.
- by-construction flags on load-bearing invariants: none. Invariant 3.3 is unstatable (its row).
- Under-specified, not cured (no rule changes, no round owed): no labelled rule says the failed list names *only* references whose create failed. Invariant 1.2 binds only the references the answer carried, Action wiring 11 and the Term failed list say what must be in the list, not what must not, and Action wiring 14 bounds it only for an empty answer; yet Check 1.4 and 1.6 read a count mismatch (a padded failed list among them) as an Invariant 1.2 violation. The Alloy model checks the rules as written and has no check for a padded list. And Invariant 3.3 has nothing to bind: Notification's record has no field that could name another record (its row).
- Result: **all Invariants covered but 3.3, which neither tool can state** — 2026-11-12. *Lineage entry: "Coverage cross-check 2026-11-12 — every Invariant but 3.3 covered by a named check, each broken by its twin; Primitive policy 7 owed."*
