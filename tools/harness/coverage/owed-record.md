## Step 2 — coverage matrix

The TLA+ model runs those calls, their refusals of a duplicate, a discharged, a flagged and a not-yet-due record, [List Owed], and the host's clock moving, over two owing patterns sharing one owed id and room for two records.

## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At `for 4 but 5 Int` (the shipped bound): all 17 checks UNSAT, all 6 runs SAT.
- At `for 5 but 5 Int` on a scratch copy: the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑ (about 40 s). Four calls already build a record flagged and then discharged beside a second record under another pattern; a fifth adds a longer history and no new shape of a record.

TLA+: two owing patterns, one owed id, room for two records, windows of 1 and 2 seconds, the clock from 1 to 3. At those bounds a record is recorded, falls due, is refused not-due, is flagged at its due instant and after it, and is discharged before and after a flag; a second [Record Owed] for one pair is tried, and two records share one owed id.

- At the shipped bound: 13,989 distinct states.
- Raised once on a scratch copy, to two owed ids and room for three records: 3,175,379 distinct states, every check holds → the same verdicts (about 146 s on 4 workers). A third record and a second owed id add states and no shape no check reads.
