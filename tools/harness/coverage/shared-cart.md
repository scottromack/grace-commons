## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At `for 3 but 4 Int, 4 Call, 7 Grant, 4 Event` (the shipped bound): all 28 checks UNSAT, all 10 runs SAT.
- At `for 3 but 4 Int, 5 Call, 8 Grant, 5 Event` (on a scratch copy outside the repo, about 2 minutes 45 s): the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑. Four calls already build every shape a check reads: an open, a share to a friend, the friend's add and a view or a history after it; an unshare of a grant of another cart; a second unshare of one grant; a change to the line's own quantity.

TLA+: at two actor references, one opened cart of two cart ids, one item, quantities 0 through 2, seven grants, two lines and five events (the shipped bound): 37,141 states. At six events: 132,069 states; at eight grants, three lines and six events: 510,085 states in about 65 s, every check holding at each (on scratch copies outside the repo). The count grows with the bound, so this is not saturation in the template's sense; it is the same verdict over every shape the larger bounds add (a third share, a third line, a sixth call). Five events already reach every shape a check reads: a denied call of every action, a refused add, change, unshare and share, an unshare after the friend's add, a clear, a view and a history. A call that commits nothing (a refusal, a denial, a view, a history) is no step in the model, since the composition keeps no record of its own (Composition state 1); each check reads the step a call that did write would be, which is how each twin is caught.
