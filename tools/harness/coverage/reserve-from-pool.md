## Step 2 — coverage matrix

The arc model has one pool, two reservations and a capacity of one, so the second reservation meets a full pool and then the slot the first gave back. A page action is one transaction (Execution Contract Database 1) and the store runs transactions one after another (Database 2), so at most one invocation is in flight; its attestation, intent entry, constituent writes and outcome entry are six separate flags (`fa`, `fi`, `fc`, `fh`, `fr`, `fo`), and the world changes at the commit step all together. The flags are separate booleans, not a position in a line, so a step that skips its predecessor is a state the order invariants can see. The binding entry, the aging of an outcome entry and the rebuild are outside every transaction and run beside an invocation. The retry model has two tokens, two action types and two digests; a call is one step per branch the page names (a replay, a collision, a recording-failure, a settle from the journal, a fresh execution), and the act's verdict is a free choice: commit, settle or refuse.

## Step 3 — bound saturation

Raise the model's scope once and confirm the explored-state count does not grow:

- Arc model, `Cap` 1 → 2: 2,101 → 3,589 states, exhaustive, holds. A third reservation passes the checker's depth cap of 40 (a hard error, not a truncation) at either capacity, since each reservation's arc is a score of steps, so the reservations stay two and the pool's second slot is the bound raised. ## Outcome

- GAP rows: none.
- by-construction flags on load-bearing invariants: none (the slot-held reading, Action wiring 118, is by construction and is not load-bearing).
- Result: clean. Three readings recorded, not counterexamples:
  - A settled answer outlives the idempotency window as a bare intent entry. A call answered pool-closed, pool-capacity-exceeded, not-held, window-elapsed or window-not-elapsed leaves its intent entry and no outcome entry (Action wiring 52, 89; Check 6.9). Once Duplicate Prevention forgets the token, a retry under it meets that intent entry and answers recording-failure carrying outcome (Action wiring 19), which tells the caller the act "reached or may have reached the act's commit and must not run again" when none did. Nothing runs twice (Invariant 5.2 holds), and a caller who wants the act again takes a fresh token; the model reaches it at `-retry-probe-forgotten-settle`.
  - Nothing says what makes the rebuild run between an auditor's lookups. Composition state 10 says the composition MUST rebuild a missing binding entry; Action wiring 80 makes a resolving action answer not-known for a reservation with none; and a replay inside the window writes nothing (Action wiring 8, 9), so Action wiring 18 repairs the binding only for a token the guard has forgotten. A Held reservation whose binding write was lost holds its slot and cannot be cancelled, confirmed or expired until the rebuild runs (`-probe-lapsed-unbound`).
