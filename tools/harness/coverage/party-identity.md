## Step 3 — bound saturation

One party, six events, five event kinds: 532 states. A longer log adds only repetitions of the same five actions, which the prefix check and `HasPassedAfterSuspend` read position by position; a run at `MaxEvents = 7` is owed to confirm the count is the only thing that grows. Not run in this pass.

Alloy (2026-11-11): `party-identity.als` checks at `for 5 but 8 Str, 3 Instant, 3 Result` (five calls in commit order and five versions: enough for enroll, verify, suspend, verify, reinstate on one party, or two parties side by side; eight strings, room for the seven id materials such a history takes; three instants, enough for a clock that goes back). A run takes about 12 minutes on a loaded machine (24 commands). Raised once on a scratch copy to `for 6 but 9 Str, 4 Instant, 3 Result` for the three history-deep checks: `A_Invariant_4_1` held (UNSAT, about 45 minutes under load); `A_Invariant_8_1` and `A_Identity_9` gave no verdict in a further 20 minutes and the run was stopped, so their saturation is not confirmed.
