## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At `for 3 but 5 Int` (the shipped bound): all 19 checks UNSAT, all 6 runs SAT. Three calls build two retentions over one record and a purge of either, which is every shape a twin needs.
- At `for 4 but 5 Int` on a scratch copy: the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑ (about 55 s). The clock reads 0 to 3 and a policy length -2 to 2, so the largest instant the model rounds, 8, stays inside 5-bit integers; a 4-bit run wrapped a purge deadline of 8 to -8 and reported a false counterexample to Invariant 6.2, and was not shipped.

TLA+: three ids, two records, two policies (durations 1 and 2, max purge delays 0 and 1), room for two retentions, a time resolution of one tick, the clock from 0 to 4. At those bounds a retention is placed, purged at its retention deadline, inside its purge window and past its purge deadline, refused early, refused as purged and as unknown, failed in storage, placed beside a sibling with another deadline over one record, and read at every now with purge eligible yes and no (each confirmed by a scratch probe).

- At the shipped bound: 84,913 distinct states.
- Raised once on a scratch copy, the clock to 5: 181,740 distinct states, every check holds → the same verdicts (about 32 s). The extra instant adds states (a clock always does) and no shape no check reads: every [Purge] answer, a late purge and a sibling short of its deadline are already reached at 4.
