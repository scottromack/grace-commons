With three clock readings (scratch copy): 28,564 states, the same verdicts, about 2 minutes. The counts grow because a third id or reading is more values the records can hold, not a new shape: two ids already reach two matches in one history ordered by occurred instant, a tie broken by id, a withdrawn communication beside an active one, and a call logged after a later exchange; two readings reach an instant later than now and a clock stepping back. The shipped bound is two so the audit's twin runs stay inside their limits.
