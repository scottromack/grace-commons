## Step 2 — coverage matrix

The Alloy model bounds: six ordered dates, three periods, up to four series for the checks that pair a series before and after a call and up to three for the rest, integers of five bits. The caps are scaled to 3 (the page's 1,000), the limit cap equal to the occurrence cap as the page has them by default. A call that writes a series is a predicate over the series before and the series after it. Twelve named `check` asserts; eighteen `run` non-vacuity predicates. Calendar arithmetic (which dates a month day, an ordinal or a week start names) is left to the conformance table; the model takes each period's picks as given.

The TLA+ model runs one series (one series id from the seam: series are records apart) through [Open], [Except], [Restore] and [End] over four range dates, the limit cap and the occurrence cap both 2, each call answered by its refuses line in order, and reads [Occurrences]' answer to every window in every state; [Open] takes the series' selected dates with the call, as the Alloy model takes the picks. It owns everything across steps; Operation 7 and 8 are checked on [Open]'s answer to every frequency, month days none, valid or out of range, and every kind of day entry with the ordinals at each edge of both ranges.

## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At 6 dates, 3 periods, 4 series (the shipped bound): all 12 checks UNSAT, all 18 runs SAT.
- At 8 dates, 4 periods, 5 series (the series-pairing checks), 4 series (the rest): the same — all 12 checks UNSAT, all 18 runs SAT; the run took twice as long and no check changed its verdict → **saturated** ☑. A series' shape is its picks, its positions, its start and its two bounds, and every distinct combination of these appears within six dates and three periods.

The TLA+ model:

- At 4 dates, caps 2 (the shipped bound): 16,897 distinct states, every check holds.
- At 5 dates, caps 2: 92,161 distinct states (2 min 42 s in TLC), every check holds. The count grows with the subsets of dates a series selects and takes out; the verdicts do not → **saturated** ☑ in verdict. Four dates hold a limit reached and one left unreached, a date taken out inside a limit's reach, a selected date before the start and a window over the cap.
