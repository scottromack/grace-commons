The TLA+ model runs [Issue], [Validate], [Revoke] (admitted, already-terminal, not-known), [Read] and the host's clock moving, over three tokens, one principal and room for two sessions.

## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At `for 4 but 5 Int` (the shipped bound): all 19 checks UNSAT, all 5 runs SAT.
- At `for 5 but 5 Int` on a scratch copy: the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑ (about 50 s). Four calls already build two sessions for one principal, one revoked after its deadline; a fifth adds a longer history and no new shape of a record.

TLA+: three tokens, one principal, room for two sessions, windows of 1 and 2 seconds, the clock from 0 to 3. At those bounds a session is issued, validated valid, expired at and after its deadline, revoked inside and after its window, re-revoked, read at every now, and a second session is issued for the same principal; a token names nothing.

- At the shipped bound: 38,108 distinct states.
- Raised once on a scratch copy, the clock to 4: 83,686 distinct states, every check holds → the same verdicts (about 29 s on 2 workers). The extra instant adds states (a clock always does) and no shape no check reads: every answer, a lapse at and past the deadline and a revoke after it are already reached at 3. A raise to three sessions and four tokens was stopped past three minutes on a shared machine and is not claimed.

## Outcome

- GAP rows: **none**. Owed: Operation 52; Operation 37, 53 and 61. Unstatable: Invariant 6.2.
- by-construction flags on load-bearing invariants: none.
- Under-specified, not cured (no rule changes, no round owed): (1) Invariant 7.1 is the host's to keep: no rule of [Issue] refuses a token a session already carries (Identity 4 bars the transition from allocating one, Invariant 7.2 binds the host), so the Alloy model takes the host's uncarried token as its input fact and the TLA+ model checks Invariant 7.2 on the host; a host that broke it would leave two sessions under one token, and what [Validate] and [Revoke] then answer ("the session token names a session") no rule settles. (2) The page sets no maximum session duration (Non-goal 28): the Alloy model holds durations and the clock to a span by a fact, so the expiry deadline is an instant it can hold. (3) The revocation fields are "immutable once set" only in their term entries; Invariant 1.2 lets a later action write them, and no labelled rule holds a written one fixed: Operation 23 refuses a re-revoke, and a refused call writes nothing (Execution Contract Read 4), which is where both models hold it. (4) Invariant 12.2's "expiry timestamp beside expiry instant" names no declared field; the Alloy model reads it as the Intent's `expired_at` column (`lapsedAt`).
- Result: **all Invariants covered** — 2026-11-11. *Lineage entry: "Coverage cross-check 2026-11-11 — every Invariant covered by a named check, each broken by its twin; Operation 52 and [Read]'s filter owed; Invariant 6.2 unstatable."*
