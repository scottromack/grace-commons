## Step 3 — bound saturation

- At `for 5 but 5 DB, 4 Call, 4 Sighting, 5 Standing, 4 Instant` on a scratch copy: the same verdicts (about 40 s) → **saturated** ☑. Three calls build every shape the page admits (an open, advanced and closed record; a recurrence; two scopes; a sighting beside a span); four add length, not shape.

- 38,566 states, about 4 s.

- Three record ids: 6,561,446 states, about 220 s, the same verdicts. A third record id adds a third span (a second recurrence) and no new kind of step: the guards read one open record per condition and scope → **saturated** ☑.
