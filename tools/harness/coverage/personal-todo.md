## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At `for 3 but 4 Call, 4 Unit` (the shipped bound): all 14 checks UNSAT, all 6 runs SAT.
- At `for 4 but 5 Call, 5 Unit` (the model as first written): the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑ (about 15 minutes under load, too slow for eighteen twins and the audit). Four calls already build every shape a check reads: a unit added, edited and completed; a description deleted and added again; two units in the list, one done; and each twin's counterexample (the longest, an edit landing after a completion, takes three calls).

TLA+: at three units, four ids and a clock of 1 to 2 (the shipped bound): 57,207 states. At four units, five ids and a clock of 1 to 3 on a scratch copy: 4,169,168 states in about 141 s, every check holding. The count grows with the bound, so this is not saturation in the template's sense; it is the same verdict over every shape the larger bound adds (a third unit beside two, a second deletion and re-add, an edit and a completion at a third instant). Three units and two instants already reach every shape a check reads: two units in the list together, a delete and a re-add of its text, an edit before a completion at a later instant.
