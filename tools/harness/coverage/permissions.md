## Step 2 — coverage matrix

The TLA+ model runs those calls with every answer each gives (granted, storage-failure; ok, not-known, not-active, storage-failure), [Check] of every pair and [Read], and the host's clock moving, over two subject references, two action scopes, three grant ids and room for two grants.

## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At `for 4 but 4 Int` (the shipped bound): all 11 checks UNSAT, all 5 runs SAT.
- At `for 5 but 4 Int` on a scratch copy: the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑. Four calls already build two grants over one pair, one revoked; a fifth adds a longer history and no new shape of a record.

TLA+: two subject references, two action scopes, three grant ids, room for two grants, the clock from 1 to 3. At those bounds every answer of every call is reached (probed), a second grant over one pair lands beside a revoked one, a pair differing in one part alone is asked, an id names nothing, and a grant is granted and revoked within one instant and across instants.

- At the shipped bound: 177,317 distinct states.
- Raised once on a scratch copy, the clock to 4: 452,169 distinct states, every check holds → the same verdicts (TLC about 72 s). A fourth instant adds longer histories and no shape no check reads.
- Raised on a scratch copy to room for three grants and four ids: stopped unfinished at 4.5 minutes, a timeout and not a pass; that bound is not claimed.

## Outcome

- GAP rows: **none**. Owed: Operation 27; String 1 through 4 and 7. Unstatable: Invariant 6.2, Identity 2, Operation 16.
- by-construction flags on load-bearing invariants: none.
- Under-specified, not cured (no rule changes, no round owed): (1) **Invariant 9.2 at a shared instant.** A grant granted and revoked at one clock reading (the Rejection paths' retry, or a grant withdrawn the second it lands) stood in active at that instant between the two calls, yet is never live at it (Term live at an instant: revocation instant after the instant). Read at any moment of an instant, Invariant 9.2 fails for a conforming build; read at the instant's end, it holds, and Check 2.1's reconstruction is exact. The page does not say which; the TLA+ model reads the end and records the reading in its header. (2) Invariant 5.1 rests on the host: the page's [Grant] refuses no reused id, and the id's freshness is the host's under Execution Contract Logic confinement 16 (open 2026-10-26-a already routes the id's source); the model takes the host's fresh id and checks the atom records it.
- Result: **all Invariants covered** but Invariant 6.2 (unstatable) — 2026-11-12. *Lineage entry: "Coverage cross-check 2026-11-12 — every Invariant but 6.2 covered by a named check, each broken by its twin; Operation 27 and String 1 through 4 and 7 owed; Invariant 9.2's instant reading a finding."*
