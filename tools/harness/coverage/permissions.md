## Step 3 — bound saturation

Alloy reports no state count, so the scope is raised once and the verdict compared:

- At `for 4 but 4 Int` (the shipped bound): all 12 checks UNSAT, all 5 runs SAT.
- At `for 5 but 4 Int` on a scratch copy (2026-11-19): the same verdicts → **saturated** ☑. Three calls already build a pair granted, revoked and granted again, and a held pair's [Grant] that records nothing; a fifth call adds a longer history and no new shape of a record.

TLA+: two subject references, two action scopes, three grant ids, room for two grants, two [Grant] calls in flight at once, the clock from 1 to 3. At those bounds every answer of every call is reached (probed 2026-11-19: a recorded grant's id, a held grant's id, storage-failure; ok, not-known, not-active, storage-failure; permitted on a pair granted again beside its revoked grant), two calls are in flight together and the second to commit answers the first's grant, a pair granted, revoked and granted again lands, a pair differing in one part alone is asked, an id names nothing, and a grant is granted and revoked within one instant and across instants.

- At the shipped bound: 868,289 distinct states (177,317 before 2026-11-19).
- Raised once on a scratch copy, the clock to 4: 2,141,561 distinct states, every check holds → the same verdicts (TLC about 65 s). A fourth instant adds longer histories and no shape no check reads.
- Raised on a scratch copy to three [Grant] calls in flight: 903,169 distinct states, every check holds (TLC about 25 s); with three ids a third call in flight adds a third rival and no shape `GC1_ConcurrentGrantsRecordOne` does not already read.
- Raised on a scratch copy to room for three grants and four ids: stopped unfinished at 4.5 minutes, a timeout and not a pass; that bound is not claimed.

## Outcome

- GAP rows: **none**. Owed: Operation 27; String 1 through 4 and 7. Unstatable: Invariant 6.2, Identity 2, Operation 16. Retired: Operation 6.
- by-construction flags on load-bearing invariants: none.
- Under-specified, not cured (no rule changes, no round owed): (1) **Invariant 9.2 at a shared instant.** A grant granted and revoked at one clock reading (the Rejection paths' retry, or a grant withdrawn the second it lands) stood in active at that instant between the two calls, yet is never live at it (Term live at an instant: revocation instant after the instant). Read at any moment of an instant, Invariant 9.2 fails for a conforming build; read at the instant's end, it holds, and Check 2.1's reconstruction is exact. The page does not say which; the TLA+ model reads the end and records the reading in its header.
