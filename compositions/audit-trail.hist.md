# Audit Trail — history

The dated record of how [Audit Trail](./audit-trail.md) got here, moved out of the spec on 2026-10-20 because a reader does not need it to use the spec today. The spec keeps every section `spec-format.md` requires and its `Deleted:` tombstones; everything smaller is in `git log -- compositions/audit-trail.md`.
