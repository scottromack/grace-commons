# Why Grace

Every business is stuck choosing between software that boxes it in and custom software that costs a fortune and never ends.

There is a third option.

## Most of your business has been built before

Logins. Orders. Approvals. Gift cards. Audit trails. Every business needs them, and every business pays to build them again – a little differently, a little wrong each time.

Grace Commons writes each one down once, in plain English anyone in the business can read, and checks it until nothing is left to find. The library holds {{ site.data.stats.patterns }} patterns today, {% if site.data.stats.patterns == site.data.stats.grounded %}every one marked done{% else %}{{ site.data.stats.grounded }} of them marked done{% endif %}. [See each one, with its status and date](./roadmap.md)

## The small part that is different is your gold

Set the common parts aside and what's left is how you work: your pricing, your approvals, your way with customers. That's your real IP.

Describe it the way you'd explain it to a new hire. Grace asks what a good developer would ask ("What if the order is cancelled after it ships?") and writes your answers down – clear, dated, in your name.

Your part stays yours. The common parts go to the commons; your edge never does unless you choose to give it. [Read the pledge](./ip-pledge.md)

## Wrong gets caught on paper, not in production

Before anything is built, every pattern is read by independent reviewers, then tested by machine. The machine walks through every situation the pattern can get into: {{ site.data.stats.states_millions }} million so far, zero failures.

Then we test the tests. We break each rule on purpose – {{ site.data.stats.twins }} times so far – and the check has to catch it. Every time, it has.

None of this asks you to trust us. The checks run in public; anyone can run them again. [See the checks run](./status.md) · [Run them yourself](./verify.md)

## Named for Grace Hopper

She first argued that business logic should be readable by the people who understand the business.
